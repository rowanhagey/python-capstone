# Customer Complaint Handling Procedure

## Overview
This document describes how customer complaints are triaged, escalated, and resolved.

## Intake
Complaints arrive through email, phone, or the support portal and are logged in the CRM within 1 hour.
Each complaint is assigned a severity level: Low, Medium, High, or Critical.
Critical complaints (service outage, data breach, billing errors over $10,000) trigger immediate escalation.

## Response Time Targets
Low severity: initial response within 2 business days.
Medium severity: initial response within 1 business day.
High severity: initial response within 4 hours.
Critical severity: initial response within 1 hour, with executive notification.

## Resolution Process
The assigned support agent investigates, proposes a resolution, and confirms with the customer.
If unresolved after 3 contact attempts, the case escalates to a Tier 2 specialist.
Refunds or credits above $500 require manager approval.

## Customer Success Follow-up
All resolved complaints receive a satisfaction survey within 24 hours of closure.
Complaint trends are reviewed monthly by the Customer Success leadership team to identify systemic issues.
