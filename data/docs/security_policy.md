# Company Security Policy

## Overview
This document outlines the security policies applicable to all employees and contractors.

## Access Control
All employees must use multi-factor authentication (MFA) for access to internal systems.
Access to production systems is restricted to the DevOps and Platform Engineering teams.
Access requests must be approved by a manager and logged in the IT ticketing system.

## Data Classification
Data is classified into three tiers: Public, Internal, and Confidential.
Confidential data (customer PII, financial records) must be encrypted at rest and in transit.
Internal data may be shared within the company but not externally without approval.

## Password Requirements
Passwords must be at least 14 characters and rotated every 90 days.
Password reuse across the last 10 passwords is prohibited.
Shared or generic accounts are not permitted except for approved service accounts.

## Incident Response
Security incidents must be reported to the Security Operations Center (SOC) within 1 hour of detection.
The incident response team follows a five-stage process: Identify, Contain, Eradicate, Recover, Review.
All incidents are documented in the post-incident review log within 5 business days.

## Device Security
All company devices must have endpoint detection and response (EDR) software installed.
Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 24 hours for remote wipe.
